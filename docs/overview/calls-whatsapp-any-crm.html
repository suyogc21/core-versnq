Calls and WhatsApp into any CRM

Versnq Core · 17 Nov 2026 · how LeadSquared and HubSpot do it, how we do it, and what's left for the LeadSquared customer.

The ask (your LeadSquared contact)

"Keep working in LeadSquared. Every call and every WhatsApp message my reps have should land on the lead as an activity there, with a summary of the call."

So the job is: capture calls + WhatsApp → summarise → write into LeadSquared. The official WhatsApp API is secondary (your call, Nov 17).

1. How LeadSquared does it today

Calls

  • Cloud telephony through partners: Exotel, Ozonetel, Ameyo, Knowlarity, CallGear, 3CX, a "universal" connector.
  • Their Android app tracks the rep's own SIM calls and SMS, with recording. iPhone: no.
  • Calls land as activities on the lead.

Summaries / AI

  • Invorto: their AI voice product, writes transcripts, summaries, intent. Paid per connected minute.
  • Call analysis report in SIERA: "a paid feature".

WhatsApp

  • Only the official WhatsApp API, through partners: Gupshup, Interakt, DoubleTick, Tata Tele and others.
  • Tracking a rep's own WhatsApp: not found in their docs.

2. How HubSpot does it

Calls

  • Built-in calling on Twilio-powered numbers (Sales / Service Hub, Starter and up).
  • Other calling apps plug in through their Calling SDK.
  • Mobile app asks the rep to log a call (Android). Not automatic.

Summaries / AI

  • Transcripts and AI call summaries: Professional / Enterprise seats only.

WhatsApp

  • Official WhatsApp Business only, in the inbox: Marketing or Service Hub Professional / Enterprise.
  • HubSpot says: "you cannot connect a personal WhatsApp account".

The gap we fill

Neither one tracks a rep's own WhatsApp. LeadSquared tracks SIM calls but charges extra for AI. HubSpot doesn't auto-log SIM calls at all. Versnq sits on top of any CRM and brings: SIM calls with recordings, the rep's own WhatsApp, and summaries, written into the CRM they already pay for.

3. How Versnq does it (the flow)

One pipeline. Calls and WhatsApp land in Core first (so nothing is lost if the CRM is down), then go to the company's CRM.

Calls

→
Versnq Android appReads the call log + picks up the recording. Unknown number? Asks the rep.
→

Cloud calling (the company's own Twilio) joins the same pipeline at "Versnq Core".

WhatsApp (the rep's own number, no API needed)

Rep's WhatsAppTheir normal number, open in WhatsApp Web on the computer.
→
Versnq Chrome extensionShows who the lead is; logs the chat's messages when the chat is opened.
→
Versnq CoreMessages on the lead's timeline, never logged twice.
→
LeadSquared / any CRM"WhatsApp (Versnq)" activity on the lead.

WhatsApp on the phone itself can't be read by another app (WhatsApp is closed and encrypted); the honest way is WhatsApp Web + our extension. The official API (company number) comes later and joins at "Versnq Core".

6. The plan, mandatory first

Me (no money needed)

  1. Forward call summaries to the CRM — built 17 Nov.
  2. Recording link in the CRM activity — built 17 Nov.
  3. Several CRMs at once (only if needed): one company sending to two places (e.g. AMS + LeadSquared).
  4. Walk-through of the whole flow on the live server; fix whatever breaks.

You

  1. Install the new app, turn on call tracking, make 2–3 calls with leads.
  2. Publish the WhatsApp extension (I'll guide you; free).
  3. Ask your contact for a LeadSquared test account (or their sandbox) + API keys. That's the only way to prove it end to end.
  4. Later, when you're ready to spend: the speech-to-text key for ACE summaries.

What the customer does on day one

  1. Their admin pastes the LeadSquared keys into Versnq → Settings → Connected CRM and clicks Test.
  2. Reps install the Versnq app (calls) and the Chrome extension (WhatsApp Web).
  3. Done: calls and WhatsApp show up on the lead in LeadSquared, with the summary once ACE is on.
