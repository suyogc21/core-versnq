WhatsApp: two lanes

A company uses one official number for campaigns and automatic replies, and each rep keeps their own WhatsApp. Both lanes land on the same lead in Core.

Lane A: the company's official number Built · waits on Meta

  • Approved templates for the first message; free text inside the 24-hour window
  • Automatic welcome and away replies; an unknown number becomes a new lead
  • STOP opts a person out; nothing automatic follows
  • WhatsApp steps in sequences; sent / delivered / read ticks
  • Payment reminders on WhatsApp
  • Goes live after Meta verifies the business. Until then it runs in test mode.

Lane B: the rep's own WhatsApp (Chrome extension) Built · works now

  • The rep keeps using their own WhatsApp (phone or Business app) through WhatsApp Web
  • A Versnq side panel shows who this person is in Core
  • One click links the chat to a lead, or creates the lead
  • The chat is saved on the lead's timeline and in the Core inbox
  • It never sends anything by itself. The rep types in WhatsApp as always.
  • Tested on a stand-in page; the first try on real WhatsApp Web is next.

Email: how it works today

Core sends from your own mailboxes (Gmail, Google Workspace, Zoho, any mail server). Replies come back into Core, so the whole conversation sits on the lead.

Each rep's own inbox Works now

  • The rep connects their mailbox once (app password)
  • Replies written in Core go out from their own address and are saved in their Sent folder
  • Emails from leads are pulled in every few minutes, onto the lead and into the shared inbox
  • Emails the rep sends from Gmail itself are captured too
  • Personal emails can be excluded

Sequences (automatic follow-ups) Works · one company mailbox

  • Steps: email, WhatsApp, wait, task; sending hours and a daily cap
  • Open pixel and tracked links; one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo rules)
  • Stops on reply, bounce or unsubscribe; suppression list honoured
  • Today all sequence emails go from one company mailbox. Sending from each rep's inbox, with rotation, is the plan (tab 6).

About open tracking (the "pixel"): Core already adds the pixel and tracked links. But opens are approximate everywhere, Smartlead included: Apple Mail and Gmail load images on their own servers. For cold email, many senders turn tracking off because pixels and tracked links can hurt inbox placement. Core can't switch tracking off per sequence yet; that's on the plan (tab 6). Replies are the signal that counts, and those are exact.
About Amazon SES: if every campaign goes from the customer's own connected inboxes (like Smartlead), we don't need SES for campaigns. SES would only be needed for Core's own system emails (invites, password resets) and copy-in addresses. A small transactional provider or a Versnq mailbox can cover those.

Us vs Smartlead

Smartlead is a sending machine with a basic inbox; a CRM must be wired to it. Core is the CRM, so every email, WhatsApp, call, deal and payment is on one timeline. Where we're behind is high-volume cold sending.

What's next (proposed)

A revised email plan, so we can honestly say "we send from your inboxes, like Smartlead, and the trail is in your CRM". These are proposals to approve; none of them is built yet.

E1 Send from each rep's own inbox

A sequence email goes out from the lead owner's connected mailbox, with a daily limit per inbox and rotation across the team's inboxes.

E2 Tracking switch + own tracking domain

Tracking off by default for cold sequences. When on, links use the customer's own domain (e.g. track.theircompany.com).

E3 Warm-up and inbox health

Use a warm-up partner beside Core; Core shows each inbox's bounces and replies and pauses a sick inbox.

E4 System emails without SES

Invites and password resets through a small transactional provider or a Versnq mailbox, not campaign traffic.
